Black Friday and Cyber Monday (BFCM) aren’t just for retail businesses. If you specialize in a service, you too can take advantage of the shopping rush leading up to the holidays. By sharing your offering as another way for your customers to save money, give gifts, or prepare for the next year, you can also boost your sales during the BFCM season.
