If you run a health clinic, chances are you’re always looking for ways to connect with your patients better. Strong patient connections help you maintain appointment attendance, encourage patients to follow their treatment plans, and build trusting relationships. SMS’s speed and convenience make it a great tool for staying connected with your health clinic patients.
